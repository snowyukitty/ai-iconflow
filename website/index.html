A decision workflow, not a converter

Design. Review. Ship.
Then learn.

Generators make options. Converters make files. IconFlow makes the design decision visible, repeatable, and auditable.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Record the user job, essence, palette, clichés, and exact targets.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Force four different concepts and give the strongest one an ownable device.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Bake finalists into one silhouette-first sheet. Choose shape before polish.

  4. 04

    Inspect

    Review native pixels, alpha, crops, light/dark surfaces, and real targets.

  5. 05

    Ship

    Build only when automated QA is clean and every human score is at least 4/5.

  6. 06

    Learn

    Turn every failed pass into a reusable casebook rule for the next icon.
