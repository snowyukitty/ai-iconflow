## Summary
A single side-view koi turns a daily habit into upstream return; three glass panes and one edge-open wake replace the familiar paired-fish emblem.

## What failed first
The first dark card used leading at nearly the same value as its field. At both
128 px and 16 px the fish body broke into an orange tail, red tile, and tiny
teal fragment. Moving the panes onto a pale glass field restored one continuous
side-view noun and kept the two-pixel upstream cut visible.

## Lessons
<!-- One reusable rule per bullet. `- [ ]` = not yet distilled into the docs;
     flip to `- [x]` after promoting it (see docs/EVOLUTION.md). -->
- [x] Dark stained-glass leading needs a contrasting field; when leading and card share a value, the semantic body breaks into decorative fragments.
