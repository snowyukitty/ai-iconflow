## Summary
A side-view walking dog is drawn as the route itself, with a tilted coral clasp providing the one semantic accent instead of a paw or map pin.

## What failed first
The leaner finalist made the back and tail feel like separate line fragments at
16 px. The promoted contour keeps the lifted tail, open belly, four-step stride,
and rotated clasp in one continuous route, raising distinctiveness without
adding detail.

## Lessons
<!-- One reusable rule per bullet. `- [ ]` = not yet distilled into the docs;
     flip to `- [x]` after promoting it (see docs/EVOLUTION.md). -->
- [x] A transparent line mark can carry a specific animal when the belly counter, stride, and lifted tail are preserved as large structural gaps.
