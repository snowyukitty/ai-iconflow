## Summary
An original cel-shaded persona is named by an asymmetric hair crest, side profile, and high collar rather than by imitated franchise features.

## What failed first
The alternate visor dominated the small render and pushed the noun toward a
generic helmet. The winning teal profile lets the uneven hair crest own the
outer edge; the face plane may disappear while the character and high collar
remain identifiable.

## Lessons
<!-- One reusable rule per bullet. `- [ ]` = not yet distilled into the docs;
     flip to `- [x]` after promoting it (see docs/EVOLUTION.md). -->
- [x] At icon scale, an original character needs an ownable head-and-collar silhouette before it needs a face.
