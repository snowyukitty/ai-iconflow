## Summary
The v0.8.3 tray mark was recognizable only as a small purple silhouette and no longer matched Yukoe's white listening-puff app icon. Three evolutions compared faithful framing, an aggressive listening-ear crop, and a dark inset; the close-cropped faithful mascot won because it enlarged the face and ear without changing brand identity.

## What failed first
The previous purple treatment discarded the master icon's white face, dark outline, and pink ear hierarchy. At the real 16 px target it read as a small status dot, even though its transparent facial cuts survived. A dark inset alternative was too thin and weak, while the maximum ear crop displaced the face. Tightening the crop around the complete white mascot restored brand fidelity and increased occupied area without sacrificing the alpha-derived template.
