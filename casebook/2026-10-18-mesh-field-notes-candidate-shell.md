## Summary
Favicon for an editorial research site that judges whether AI-generated 3D models are usable. Essence: candidate. A vermilion egg stands for a sealed shell (a candidate, not yet an asset); one off-axis pentagonal hole cut clean through it is the open mesh edge, rimmed in chartreuse the way an inspection tool highlights boundary edges. A device-free egg control read as a generic blob, so the faceted hole carries the identity.

## What failed first
The bake-off killed three directions before review. A half-faceted pebble
(smooth left, three straight facets right) blacked out to a plain round blob:
the facets lived only in colour. A rubber stamp read as a chess pawn and its
ink handle vanished on the dark tab. An edge-open chip bitten out of the egg's
upper right fell inside the collision radius of several round emoji faces at
16px. The first enclosed-hole draft used a ~216x260-unit hole with a 68-unit
band, which shrank to one light pixel at 16px; enlarging the hole to
272x288 units, the band to 96 units and the egg to 768x896 units kept an
open counter at 16px on white, dark and grey before the master was written,
so review passed on its first pass. Residual weakness: on white, the light
hole and the chartreuse band merge into one pale spot at 16px; on dark and
grey the hole reads clearly.
