## Summary
A quiet background-agent tray icon built around a mailbox-on-post silhouette. The raised amber delivery flag communicates newly observed work while the broad navy body stays calm and legible at notification-area sizes.

## What failed first
The alternate telegraph and lantern silhouettes became a gavel and a padlock at
16 px, weakening both legibility and semantic fit. The first mailbox draft also
lost the flag post against the body. Keeping the mailbox body broad and coloring
the complete raised flag amber produced the clearest small-size read.
