## Summary
Forest Familiar makes a return-home guide feel watched over by a carved horned
owl. The final full-body relief field uses unequal ear tufts, a broad breast,
and a split tail as species cues; two edge-open wing gouges add the woodcut
grammar without carrying the owl noun.

## What failed first
The pine marten reduced to a generic running quadruped and scored 2 for
legibility/scalability. A first side-view owl then read as angular origami.
The third pass moved the identifying traits onto the outer relief field and
enlarged both edge-open cuts; the owl remained named at 16 px while the cuts
continued to read as carved recesses rather than feathers or facial detail.

## Lessons
<!-- One reusable rule per bullet. `- [ ]` = not yet distilled into the docs;

- [x] For a woodcut animal, the species must live in the outer relief field before the gouges are added; carved recesses describe the medium, not the noun.
