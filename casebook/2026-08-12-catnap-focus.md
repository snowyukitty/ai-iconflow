## Summary
A sleeping cat becomes a focus timer through one curled-body posture; the broad tail-to-cheek wedge communicates settling without a clock or paw badge.

## What failed first
The first sleeping head was almost circular, so the native 16 px render could
be named as a monkey or a generic curled pet. Enlarging two triangular ears
changed the outer contour by more than one output pixel while preserving the
broad tail-to-cheek wedge.

## Lessons
<!-- One reusable rule per bullet. `- [ ]` = not yet distilled into the docs;

- [x] For curled animals, species cues must change the outer silhouette; facial details cannot rescue a round head at 16 pixels.
