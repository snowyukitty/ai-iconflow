## Summary
Personal media collector app icon: a purple hopper-vault that funnels into a keep, with one amber gather-bar as the signature. Chosen over media-deck, catch-pouch, and gem concepts for silhouette specificity and 16px legibility.

## What failed first
- First divergent pass risked the monogram trap (an "S") and the category download-arrow; those were rejected before SVG finalists.
- Media-deck looked attractive in color but silhouetted as a generic document/list app (distinctiveness ≤3).
- Catch-pouch was friendly but weaker on the "gather" verb at 16px.
- Ingather-gem facets muddied the silhouette into a trophy/person shape.
- Hopper-vault won bake-off; pass 2 only tightened optical centering and the vault door counter so 16/32px still reads funnel + keep.

- [x] **Rejected as a rule (2026-10-18):** a category motif, not a reusable construction; its general half is L1/L31 (one signature, not a second glyph). For gather/collect apps, a hopper-into-vault silhouette beats download arrows and monograms; keep the amber gather-bar as a single signature, not a second overlapping glyph.
