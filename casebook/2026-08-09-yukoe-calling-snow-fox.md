## Summary
A local-first multilingual voice companion needed one coherent identity across app, favicon, PWA, and tray. Three mascot directions reached the bake-off; the calling snow-fox profile won because its two ears, long muzzle, and open call remain a specific silhouette without relying on a generic waveform or speech bubble.

## What failed first
The curled finalist became a crescent moon or snail in the black silhouette,
while the front-facing mask changed noun from fox to owl at small sizes. The
calling profile kept the correct noun, but its first review pass let the outer
48-unit outline consume too much of the nominal 128-unit mouth gap, and the long
muzzle pushed the idea-carrying jaws past the maskable safe circle. Shortening
the muzzle from x=952 to x=900 and widening the open contour to 176 units made
both jaws deliberate in the 16/32px proof and lifted balance from 3 to 4 without
shrinking the mascot or adding detail.

## Lessons

- [x] When a profile mascot carries its verb in an open contour, budget the clear gap after outline stroke and shorten the muzzle until both jaws remain inside the maskable safe circle. (Covered by L15/L20 and the playbook's safe-area review.)
