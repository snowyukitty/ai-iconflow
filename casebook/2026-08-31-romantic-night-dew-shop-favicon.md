## Summary
A romantic night-market storefront needed its name, よろしく + 店 with the ateji 夜露店, to feel like a nocturnal rendezvous rather than a mascot. A moonlit shop eave and one warm dew pearl won because their separated, off-axis relationship stays graceful and visible at the exact 16 px target.

## What failed first

**Acceptance reset — emotional fit failed.** The predecessor lantern-shell
snail scored at least 4 on every formal axis and passed a useful device-free
control, yet the owner rejected it as unattractive and tonally wrong. It owned
an unhurried mascot story instead of the romantic nocturnal meeting encoded in
the name. The new brief therefore changed the essence from `discover` to
`rendezvous` and explicitly named `romantic`, `nocturnal`, `graceful`, and
`intimate` before drawing another path.

**Pass 1 — legibility 3, distinctiveness 3.** A dew-on-grass-blade silhouette
became a diagonal wand or exclamation construction at favicon size. A symmetric
dew-and-noren hybrid held the palette but became a spade or flame. A bare drop
control confirmed that palette alone could not stop a water-app reading. The
winning family shifted from a container metaphor to a separated relationship:
shop shelter above, night dew below.

**Pass 2 — scalability 3.** A wind-swept droplet escaped the spade silhouette
but still read as fragrance or water. Early eaves approached an umbrella or
eyebrow when their lower curve and pearl crowded together. Widening the air gap
past 128 source units, moving the 196-unit pearl right of center, and simplifying
the upper path to one rising brush-like eave made the two shapes poised rather
than pictographic.

**Final — zero warnings.** The 40-unit indigo keyline survives the exact 16 px
and 32 px ICO frames. At larger app targets, the outline disappears naturally
into the indigo night ground, leaving a moonlit lilac eave and warm amber pearl.
White, dark, and mid-gray grounds; pixel zooms; alpha and visual silhouettes;
safe-zone, circle, squircle, and rounded crops; Apple touch; and the maskable
asset were inspected. The managed Review Lab browser exposed no backend, so the
documented static-and-exact-target fallback is recorded rather than an
interactive-pass claim.
