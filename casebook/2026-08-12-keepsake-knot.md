## Summary
A shared-ritual icon uses two unequal woven bands and one offset counter, turning relationship into visible interdependence without using a heart or linked-circle shorthand.

## What failed first
The first crossing placed most of the teal mass in one upright loop. At 16 px
that loop read as a power glyph and distinctiveness fell to 3. Rebalancing the
coral return and teal fold around one offset 128-unit counter restored two
participants without closing the woven underpass.

## Lessons
<!-- One reusable rule per bullet. `- [ ]` = not yet distilled into the docs;
     flip to `- [x]` after promoting it (see docs/EVOLUTION.md). -->
- [x] When a woven loop resembles a power glyph, distribute color and direction across both participants so the crossing reads as a relationship.
