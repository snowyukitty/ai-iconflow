## Summary
Yukoe needed a much cuter identity for a local-first translation, voice, and
word-learning companion without copying an existing mascot. The winning mark
is an original round snow-puff whose oversized pink shell ear turns the product
verb, listening, into the silhouette. It won because the asymmetry stays clear
at 16 px while the soft face, blush, and scalloped body retain warmth at larger
desktop and web sizes.

## What failed first
The first two front-facing finalists used paired pointed ears. They remained
legible, but the visual-silhouette row read as a generic cat head, holding
distinctiveness at 2 even though craft and color were acceptable. A profile
alternative read as a bird. Replacing both ear tips with one large side shell
removed the animal ambiguity and made the listening cue occupy several pixels
at 16 px; the full-color raster then read as a friendly listener rather than a
category-default mascot.

## Lessons
