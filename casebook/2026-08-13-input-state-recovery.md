## Summary
A compact recovery utility needed to read as input-specific without literal key labels or generic reset symbols. A perspective keycap won after its recovery gesture moved from the top edge to one broad lateral release tab that survives at 16 px.

## What failed first
The first 16 px bake-off scored 3 for legibility and distinctiveness: the wire
puller became headphones, the exposed switch became an upload arrow, and the
open keywell became a tent. Keeping the perspective keycap restored the input
noun, but a top-mounted coral tab still read as a chimney. Moving that tab to the
side created a lateral release gesture while preserving one dominant silhouette.

## Lessons
- [x] When a tiny utility mark needs both a noun and an action, keep the noun as one dominant silhouette and place the action on a side edge; top accents readily turn into chimneys or upload arrows.
