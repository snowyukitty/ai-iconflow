## Summary
Sky Courier turns spoiler-free anticipation into a hand-carried dispatch. The
winning wing-satchel concept beat a courier swallow because the satchel stayed a
specific object at native 16 px while its flap and one broad dry cut supplied
the flying motion without a star, wand, play button, or calendar.

## What failed first
The swallow finalist was attractive at 128 px but its long diagonals collapsed
into an abstract flying glyph at 16 px, dropping legibility and scalability to
3. Promoting the prop to the main noun, nesting the seal-colored bag inside one
ink mass, and keeping a single two-pixel edge-open cut restored the courier
reading without adding detail.

## Lessons

- [x] When an original-story icon loses a creature noun at 16 px, fuse the narrative action into a concrete prop; an object-metaphor can carry more story than a generic flying silhouette.
