## Summary
A pressure-free traveling-market storefront needed a more playful mark that stayed calm at purchase time. A lantern-shell snail won because it joins the site's night-market light with its take-your-time invitation in one nameable object, and because the exact 16 px target still preserves both readings.

## What failed first

**Pass 1 — distinctiveness 2.** Four concept lenses deliberately avoided the
existing noren. The curio moth looked considered at 128 px but collapsed into a
generic butterfly or bow at 16 px. A paper-lantern control approached a bell or
capsule, repeating the category collision already recorded by the predecessor
case. The lantern koi was the strongest first-pass mark: it stayed readable at
16 px, but its visual silhouette was still simply a common fish. The lantern
ribs changed the interior story without owning the outer shape.

**Pass 2 — distinctiveness 5.** The lantern snail joined two product truths that
the shop already carried: a traveling night-market light and an explicit
take-your-time posture. Its large circular shell, long grounded foot, and paired
raised feelers stayed nameable in the silhouette strip. More importantly, the
same snail with a plain shell was baked beside it as a control. That control was
only a generic snail; adding two 42-unit curved ribs and broad top and bottom caps
created the specific chōchin-shell reading. The control isolated the signature
device instead of letting polish or palette take credit for it.

**Final — zero warnings.** The first lantern cap path carried an unnecessary
18-unit outline that triggered the small-stroke audit. Converting the already
solid cap geometry to fill-only removed the warning without changing the visible
target. The exact 16 px and 32 px ICO frames, touch icon, maskable asset, three
review backgrounds, pixel zooms, alpha footprint, silhouette strip, and adaptive
crops were inspected. The managed Review Lab browser had no available backend,
so the documented static-and-exact-target fallback was recorded rather than an
interactive-pass claim.
