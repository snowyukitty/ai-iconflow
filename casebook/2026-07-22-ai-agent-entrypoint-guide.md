## Summary
A web guide for understanding and deploying three process-scoped Codex accounts. A patch-cable splitter beat a cactus-like railway turnout and a generic dark route token because its black silhouette names a specific routing object: three keyed connectors converging into one trunk.

## What failed first
The first promoted splitter had three readable connector heads at 16/32 px,
but the centre head floated above the Y-shaped cable body. Mechanical QA stayed
clean and color looked finished, yet the geometry contradicted the product
promise that all three launch surfaces join the same route; craft was therefore
3/5. Adding a raster-aligned centre rail behind the side branches connected the
topology, and a final 0.9 scale/translation pass restored maskable safe-area
room without losing the two-pixel cyan selected-port inset.
